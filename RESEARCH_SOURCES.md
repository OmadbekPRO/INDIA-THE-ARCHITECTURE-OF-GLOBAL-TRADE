### WTO Key Facts
- **Membership:** Founding member since January 1, 1995
- **Key disputes:** DS456 (Solar), DS430 (Poultry), DS582/584/588 (ICT Tariffs), DS541 (Export Subsidies)
- **Positions:** Advocates for developing country interests, Peace Clause for food security, TRIPS flexibility

### FTP 2023 Key Facts
- **Effective:** April 1, 2023 (open-ended, dynamic framework)
- **Export target:** $2 trillion by 2030
- **Four pillars:** Incentive to Remission, Export Promotion through Collaboration, Ease of Doing Business, Emerging Areas
- **Key schemes:** Advance Authorisation, EPCG, RoDTEP, RoSCTL, Star Status Holder, Districts as Export Hubs

## Data Caveats
1. Import ranks 6–10 are estimates from cross-referenced sources
2. GDP ranking oscillates 5th/6th vs UK depending on exchange rates
3. FDI figures differ by methodology (DPIIT total vs RBI BoP-based)
4. India-UK FTA and India-EU FTA are very recent (2025-2026) developments
5. FTP 2023 is open-ended (no fixed end date, unlike previous 5-year FTPs)
6. GDP growth estimates range 6.5–7.0% depending on NSO revision vs IMF
