# PROJECT BRIEF — INDIA: THE ARCHITECTURE OF GLOBAL TRADE

**Subject:** "So'rovnoma asosida amaliy-tahliliy topshiriq: Mamlakatlar misolida tashqi iqtisodiy aloqalarni xalqaro tartibga solish tahlili"

**Duration:** 15–18 minutes (target: 17 minutes)
